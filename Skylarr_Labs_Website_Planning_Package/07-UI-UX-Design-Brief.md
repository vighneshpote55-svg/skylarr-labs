# UI/UX Design Brief
## Challenge
Turn a detailed pharma franchise offer into a clear, credible, mobile-friendly story that helps prospective partners understand the opportunity and enquire.

## Design principle
Trust first, clarity second, conversion third. Avoid pressure tactics and unsupported claims.

## Reference palette
Emerald Ink `#064E3B` and Champagne `#F8E7C9` from the supplied image. Use Emerald for primary actions/headings and Champagne as an accent with white and soft sage.

## Content hierarchy
1. What is the opportunity? 2. Who is the company? 3. What does a partner receive?
4. How does joining work? 5. Who is eligible? 6. Products/support?
7. What do real partners say? 8. What happens after enquiry?

## Hero
Compact header, strong headline, brief supporting text, “Get Franchise Details” primary CTA, “Watch Company Introduction” secondary CTA, approved founder/team/company visual, verified proof only. Stack on mobile.

## Images and videos
Use authentic founder/team, office, warehouse, dispatch, actual product packs, training, partner meetings/events, and consented testimonials. Stock only as temporary/supporting visuals. Use video stills for thumbnails and confirm image rights.

## Interaction
Alternate white/sage backgrounds; concise cards; optional sticky mobile CTA that does not obstruct controls; accessible FAQ; video gallery with labels and controls. Avoid excessive animation.

## Form UX
Short form, persistent labels, clear required fields, accessible validation, consent notice, explanation of follow-up. Ask investment range only if necessary and approved.

## Deliverables
Mobile/desktop wireframes, high-fidelity key screens, component states, tokens, image cropping notes, responsive rules, engineering annotations.
